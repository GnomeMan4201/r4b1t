# r4B1T_h0L3

![r4B1T_h0L3](demo.svg)

**A StumbleUpon-style random discovery engine for OSINT, security tools, and the open web.**

31,573 URLs. No algorithm. No recommendations. No filter. Pure chaos.

---

## → [gnomeman4201.github.io/r4b1t](https://gnomeman4201.github.io/r4b1t)

---

## How it works

Click **GO ↓** — a random site surfaces. You see the domain before you go anywhere.

**VISIT ↗** opens it in a new tab. **SKIP →** rolls again.

Your trail of visited domains sits at the bottom. The counter keeps score.

---

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| `Space` or `→` | Roll a new site |
| `Enter` | Visit current preview |

---

## Onion sites

148 `.onion` addresses are in the pool. The first time one surfaces, r4B1T_h0L3 asks if you have [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/download/) installed before opening anything. If not, it skips it or excludes them from the session entirely — your call.

Drag the **⬛ r4B1T_h0L3** link that appears after your first roll to your bookmarks bar. One click from any page brings you back.
